POW Coffee privacy statement

Last updated: 20 August 2026

POW Coffee takes care with your personal data. This privacy statement sets out which data we collect, why we do so, and what rights you have.

This is a translation of the Dutch original. In the event of any difference between the two versions, the Dutch version prevails.

Who we are

POW Coffee is a trade name of a sole proprietorship based in Rotterdam.

  • Company name: POW Coffee
  • Address: Postbus 4327, 3006 AH Rotterdam, the Netherlands
  • Chamber of Commerce number: 93238509
  • VAT ID: NL005007658B67
  • Email: info@powcoffee.nl

POW Coffee is the controller for the personal data collected through this website.

Which data we collect

When you sign up through our form, we collect the following data:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address

You provide this data yourself when filling in the form.

What we use your data for

We use your data solely to:

  • Keep you up to date on developments around POW Coffee, including news about the launch;
  • Send you marketing communications such as offers and updates, but only if you have explicitly consented to this.

We do not use your data for any purpose other than those described above.

Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give that consent actively through the checkboxes in the sign-up form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing (see below).

Who we share your data with

For processing your data we currently use HubSpot as our marketing platform only. HubSpot stores and processes your data on our behalf. Our HubSpot environment is set to store data within the European Union (Germany). HubSpot may use sub-processors for certain supporting services; an up-to-date overview is published by HubSpot at hubspot.com/legal/subprocessors.

We do not share your data with any other third parties. As POW Coffee grows and new suppliers are added (for example for the webshop, shipping or accounting), we will update this privacy statement and inform you about it.

Transfers outside the EEA

We do not transfer your data to countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Should this change in future because a supplier processes data outside the EEA, we will put appropriate safeguards in place (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) and inform you in an updated version of this statement.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent, with a maximum of 24 months after your last interaction with our communications (for example opening an email). After you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, unless we are legally required to keep it longer.

Cookies

As long as you have not made a choice in the cookie banner, we place no cookies and load no third-party code.

If you accept, we load HubSpot's analytics code. That places four cookies (__hstc, hubspotutk, __hssrc and __hssc), which we use to see how the site is used. If you decline, none of that happens. You can revise your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings button at the bottom of the homepage; if you withdraw your consent, we delete those cookies immediately and do not load the code again.

Your choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage, so we do not have to ask again on every visit. It contains no personal data.

When you open the sign-up form, HubSpot loads that form. This is necessary to process your sign-up and is independent of your cookie choice.

The typeface on this site is served from our own server, not from a third party.

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Among other things, we use a secured platform (HubSpot) with encrypted data storage.

Consequences of not providing data

Providing your data through the sign-up form is voluntary. If you do not fill in (part of) the required fields, we cannot sign you up for our communications.

Minors

Our communications are not aimed at people under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the following rights:

  • Access — you can request which data we hold about you;
  • Rectification — you can have inaccurate data corrected;
  • Erasure — you can ask us to delete your data;
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent — you can unsubscribe from our communications at any time;
  • Portability — you can ask to receive your data in a portable format;
  • Lodging a complaint — you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Would you like to exercise one of these rights, or do you have a question about how we handle your data? Send an email to info@powcoffee.nl.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from our communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails, or by sending an email to info@powcoffee.nl.

Changes to this privacy statement

We may adjust this privacy statement from time to time, for example when we add new services or suppliers. The date at the top of this page indicates when the statement was last updated.
